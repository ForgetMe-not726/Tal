阅读技能
同义替换
在考试中，这种技巧通常表现为“抽象概念（题目） ↔ 具象细节（原文）”的互相转换。下面是具体问题中的应用，看看出题人是如何隐藏答案的：

[TABLE]

题目中的核心词
文章中的相似表达
替换技巧
Q1: Which teenager would rather do something practical or artistic?

Text (Teenager B): "...doing things that are creative or that make me feel useful by helping others."


artistic（艺术的）被同义替换为了 creative（有创造力的）。
practical（实用的/实践的）被具象化转述为 make me feel useful by helping others（通过帮助别人让我感到自己有用）。

Q2: Which teenager is looking forward to doing something fun?

Text (Teenager A): "...I'm going to have a big party after my exams... It's going to be great!"


题目给的是抽象的 something fun（有趣的事）。
原文给出了具体的场景 have a big party（开盛大的派对），并用 great 表达了期待。

Q3: Which teenager is worried about the financial aspect of studying?


Text (Teenager C): "...the tuition fees... are incredibly high! My parents can't afford to pay them, so I'm already saving up... get a student loan..."


tuition fees（学费）、can't afford（付不起）、saving up（存钱）、student loan（学生贷款）。读懂这组“词汇家族”是解题关键。
Q4: Which teenager disagrees with their family about their future?


Text (Teenager B): "...they want me to do a science degree... but I don't think it's for me. I've decided I'm going to become a music teacher..."


题目用了一个动词 disagrees with（不同意/有分歧）。
原文并没有出现 disagree 这个词，而是通过逻辑转折句型表达了分歧：“他们想让我做……但是(but) 我觉得那不适合我”。

 Q5: Which teenager is pleased with their improved school performance?
Text (Teenager A): "I'm quite positive I'll get good marks... He explains stuff a lot more clearly... I now feel much more self-confident thanks to him."

pleased (满意的/高兴的) 被转述成更具体的积极情绪，即 positive (乐观的) 和 self-confident (自信的)。
improved school performance (提升的学业表现) 在原文中没有用一个具体的名词来对应，而是通过因果逻辑来体现的：因为家教“讲得更清楚 (explains clearly)”，所以学生“确信能拿高分 (get good marks)”，这一连串的状态变化，综合起来就等同于“学业表现得到了提升”。
Q6:  Which teenager is keen on the idea of travelling for work in the future?

Text (Teenager C): "My dream is to become a doctor and travel around the world helping people in troubled areas."


keen on the idea（热衷于这个想法）转述为 My dream is...（我的梦想是……）。
travelling for work（为工作而旅行）被转述为 travel around the world helping people（作为医生环游世界去救人，即工作）。

复习：表将来的时态和结构
英语里表达"将来"没有固定的单一时态。说话人会根据计划的确定程度、决定的时间点、以及判断的依据，灵活选用不同的时态和结构。
