## Hello World

In the UK, around 80 percent of people live in towns and cities. Most of the other 20 percent live in villages. Villages are smaller and less crowded than towns and cities, and some people prefer living in them because they can have a healthier, more relaxed lifestyle.

![](images/c067d4117be6aceb8ef5e5f7aadf4fbff587527fe25ec73e76a0ab415ef32d6f.jpg)

![](images/ded0e9beee4f217069e53bbb9ba6cb5c37451571e75d5d90d0f290d58bdd59fe.jpg)

For centuries, most people in the UK lived in villages and earnt a living from farming. However, after the steam engine was invented, more and more factories were built. In the late 18th and early 19th century, a lot of people in the UK left their villages and moved to big cities so that they could find work in the factories, producing goods such as cotton and metals. This period in history is known as the Industrial Revolution.

Nowadays, some people choose to live in a village even though they work in a city. They often have long, stressful journeys to work every day, but at the weekend they can relax and enjoy the clean air and the peace and quiet of the countryside.

## Question tags

## 4Read and practise the examples in pairs.

Question tags are short questions at the end of a sentence. We use them:

to check information we're not sure about (our voice goes up)

You've been to Spain before, haven't you? (= I'm not sure if you've ever been to Spain.)

to ask someone to agree with us (our voice goes down).

The lake is beautiful, isn’t it? (= I think the lake is beautiful. I’m sure you agree.)
