## Did you know...?

All human beings have their own personality – in other words, their own way of thinking, behaving, feeling and responding to experiences in life. Some of the most important factors that shape our personality are family, school, the culture we live in and important life events.

According to research, every baby is unique and is born with its own personality traits. As we grow up, our personality develops and it starts to be noticeable as early as four years old. Although events that take place in a person's life can affect their personality and change it, the basic traits stay the same. So, for example, if a person is a talkative extrovert as a child, they will probably be like that for the rest of their life. This doesn't mean that a person can't change the way they think and behave, although it will take a lot of hard work and determination. If we think about our strengths

and weaknesses carefully, we can begin to develop our positive qualities and control our negative ones.

R: Well, Paul – that's my friend – and I met when we were in Year 1 of primary school. We shared a desk and even then he was quite a character, but we didn't get along at first. Although he was good company and a really imaginative kid, he also used to be quite bad-tempered and arrogant. He used to laugh at me because I was very naive and I would believe everything he said. But, anyway, we became close friends and when I saw him last week, I realised how important childhood friendships are.

P: Yes, I have a few childhood friends who are always there for me. We care about each other and we appreciate each other's honesty.

R: That's important. A friend who is by your side your whole life must be trustworthy and sympathetic about your problems. They shouldn't just think about themselves – selfishness is a bad quality in anyone, especially a friend.

## Words with multiple meanings

In English, there are many words that change meaning depending on what type of word they are (noun or verb) and depending on what other words are around them.

For example:

My cousin Ed is a real character. / The character I play in the film is a successful businesswoman. / Mary would never lie. It’d be out of character.
